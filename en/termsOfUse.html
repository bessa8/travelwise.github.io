Description of the service

TravelWise is a trip planning and management app. It lets you create trips with one or several cities, organise the itinerary by day, record and split expenses, keep a journal and share the trip with other people. It includes an optional artificial-intelligence suggestion feature (“AI itinerary”), paid in credits.

TravelWise is not a travel agency. It does not sell or book trips, accommodation, transport or tickets, and has no integration with airlines, rail operators or booking platforms.

In a shared trip, members see and, if they are collaborators, edit that trip’s content. Share only what you have the right to share.

AI itinerary

  • AI suggestions are proposals, not guarantees. They may contain errors, closed venues, outdated opening hours or inaccurate estimates.
  • The AI has no access to real timetables, availability or today’s prices. The values it shows are estimates, and are marked as such.
  • Always confirm times, prices, access conditions and availability with the official bodies before you travel.
  • The AI does not book, does not buy and does not change your itinerary without your choice.
  • The list of what the AI does and does not do is on the home page and in the app itself.
