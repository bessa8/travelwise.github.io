Data controller

The controller of the personal data processed through the TravelWise application (the “app”) and this website is Our Small World (“we”).

For any privacy question or to exercise your rights, contact us at oswtraveladm@gmail.com.

This policy applies to the TravelWise app for iOS and Android and to the travelwise.pt website.

International transfers

Some providers (Google, OpenAI, Apple, Unsplash and, depending on the region, Supabase) may process data outside the European Economic Area, notably in the United States. These transfers rely on the EU-US Data Privacy Framework, where the provider is certified, or on standard contractual clauses approved by the European Commission.

Retention periods

  • Account and trip data: while the account exists. When you delete the account, they are erased (see Delete account and data).
  • Shared trips: if you delete your account and a trip of yours has other active members, the trip passes to one of them and is not deleted.
  • Purchase records: for the period required by tax law.
  • Crash reports and statistics: up to 24 months, then anonymised.
  • Support emails: for as long as needed to reply and meet legal obligations.

Delete account and data

You can delete your account and associated data at any time in the app, under Settings → Delete account. You can also request it by email at oswtraveladm@gmail.com, from the email address linked to the account.

  • Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.
  • Unused credits are lost and not refunded.
  • To avoid being charged again, cancel the Premium subscription in the App Store or Google Play.
  • Records we are legally required to keep (for example, tax records) are kept for the legal period.

Your rights

Under the GDPR, you have the right to:

  • access the data we hold about you;
  • rectification of inaccurate data (most can be corrected in the app itself);
  • erasure (you can delete your account in the app or by email);
  • portability: receive the data you gave us in a structured format;
  • object to processing based on legitimate interest;
  • restriction of processing;
  • withdraw consent at any time, without affecting processing already carried out (for example, by withdrawing permissions in the system settings).

To exercise any right, write to oswtraveladm@gmail.com from the email linked to the account. We reply within one month at most, extendable under the GDPR.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children of that age. A trip may include minor travellers (for example, children’s ages to adapt the itinerary), registered by an adult who is responsible for that data. If you know a minor created an account without authorisation, contact us and we will remove the data.

Security

  • Connections between the app and the servers are encrypted (HTTPS/TLS).
  • Access to each trip is controlled in the database by per-user rules, not only in the app.
  • Sensitive data, such as date of birth, has column-level restricted access.

No system is infallible. If we detect a data breach that poses a risk to you, we will inform you and the supervisory authority, as required by law.

Cookies and this website

The travelwise.pt website does not use cookies, analytics or tracking scripts. The only external resource is Google Fonts typefaces. Your light/dark theme preference, if you change it, is stored only in your browser (local storage) and is never sent to us.

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.), which may log technical access data, such as the IP address, for security and service operation.

Complaints

If you believe the processing of your data breaches the GDPR, you can lodge a complaint with the Portuguese data protection authority, the Comissão Nacional de Proteção de Dados (CNPD), at www.cnpd.pt, or with the authority of your country of residence. We would appreciate it if you contacted us first, so we can try to resolve the issue.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app or the law changes. The date at the top shows the version in force. If the change is significant, we will tell you in the app before it takes effect.
